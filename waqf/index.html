Al-Zij is a waqf for the sake of Allah

Al-Zij is an astronomical clock: time, prayer times, Qibla direction, the Hijri and Gregorian calendars, and the Moon, Sun and sky. It is a waqf — an endowment dedicated for the sake of Allah.

What that means

  • Completely free, forever: no subscription, no fees, no paid version.
  • No ads and no promotional notifications, ever.
  • Keeps no information about you: no account, no sign-up, no personal questions.
  • No commercial use, and no data is ever sold.

Accuracy first

Saudi prayer times come from the official Umm al-Qura calendar table itself, and the Hijri date matches it. Al-Zij also checks itself daily against independent sources.
