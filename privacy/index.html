خدمات عامة مجانية يتصل بها جهازك مباشرة

لعرض أشياء إضافية، يطلب جهازك المعلومة مباشرة من خدمات عامة مجانية، دون أن تمر بالزيج. هذه الخدمات ترى عنوان الإنترنت (IP) والإحداثيات المرسلة في الطلب، ولكل منها سياسة خصوصية خاصة بها:

  • الطقس وجودة الهواء والارتفاع والبحث عن المدن: Open-Meteo.
  • اسم المدينة والحي: OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) و BigDataCloud.
  • الموقع التقريبي عند عدم السماح بالموقع الدقيق: ipwho.is و ipapi.co و GeoJS (تقدّر المدينة من عنوان الإنترنت).
  • التحقق اليومي من دقة المواقيت: Aladhan و sunrise-sunset.org.

الزيج لا يستقبل هذه الطلبات ولا يحفظها، ولا يربطها بك. وإذا لم يتوفر الإنترنت يعمل الزيج بدونها.

في التطبيق والموقع معًا

عند الإبلاغ عن مشكلة

زر «عندي مشكلة» يفحص الزيج على جهازك، ثم يعرض التقرير كاملًا قبل الإرسال، ولا يُرسل شيء إلا حين تضغط «أرسل» بنفسك. يحتوي التقرير على:

  • نوع الجهاز والمتصفح، والإعدادات، ونتائج الفحص؛
  • موقعك مقرّبًا إلى نحو كيلومتر، واسم المدينة والحي كما يظهران لك؛
  • سجل ما حدث بلا أسماء أحياء.

لا اسم فيه ولا رقم. يفتح تطبيق البريد والتقرير فيه، فيصلنا من بريدك أنت، ونستعمله لحل المشكلة والرد عليك فقط.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026
In short: Al-Zij collects no information about you. There is no account or sign-up, we never ask for your name, age or gender, and there are no ads, no tracking and no analytics. All calculations run on your phone; Al-Zij has no server that receives your data.

📱 The Al-Zij iPhone app

This part is about the Al-Zij app from the App Store only.

What stays on your phone only

Your settings, your last location (so prayer times and the Qibla work offline), your calendar reminders, the optional adhan log, and the internal self-check log. None of this is sent to us, and deleting the app removes all of it.

Location

The app needs your location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction; the calculation happens on your phone. If you do not allow location, you choose your city yourself; it is never estimated from your IP address.

City and district names

From Apple's map service on the iPhone.

Compass and motion sensors

Used only to show the Qibla direction; read on your phone and never sent anywhere.

Adhan alerts

Scheduled on your phone, with the system's permission, for the coming days, so they reach you while the phone is locked. None comes from a server, and we never send promotional notifications.

Updates

The app fetches the latest Al-Zij page from alzijapp.com and sends no data about you; the host sees your IP address, as with any website.

No other service

The app contacts no other service on its own. Searching for a city by name looks in the city list on your phone, then in Apple Maps.

🌐 The Al-Zij website, alzijapp.com

This part is about Al-Zij when you open it in a browser.

What stays on your device only

The same as in the app. None of it is sent to us, and clearing the site's data in your browser removes all of it.

Location

Al-Zij needs your location to calculate prayer times and the Qibla direction. The calculation happens on your device. If you do not allow precise location, Al-Zij uses an approximate one, and you can lock your location.

Free public services your device contacts directly

For extra information your device requests it directly from free public services, without passing through Al-Zij. These services see your IP address and the coordinates in the request, and each has its own privacy policy: weather, air quality, elevation and city search — Open-Meteo; city and district names — OpenStreetMap (Nominatim) and BigDataCloud; approximate location when precise location is not allowed — ipwho.is, ipapi.co and GeoJS; daily prayer-time accuracy check — Aladhan and sunrise-sunset.org. Al-Zij does not receive, store or link these requests to you, and works without them when offline.

Compass and motion sensors

Used only to show the Qibla direction; read on your device and never sent anywhere.

Notifications

Adhan alerts and reminders are scheduled on your device. We never send server or promotional notifications.

Hosting

The Al-Zij website is hosted on GitHub Pages; like any website host, it sees your IP address when the page loads, and we have no access to it.

In the app and on the website

Problem reports

The “I have a problem” button checks Al-Zij on your device, then shows the whole report before sending; nothing is sent until you tap Send. The report has your device and browser type, settings and check results; your location rounded to about a kilometre and the city and district names as you see them; and a log of what happened, without district names. It holds no name or number. Your email app opens with the report in it, so it reaches us from your own email, and we use it only to fix the problem and reply to you.

Children

Al-Zij collects no information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

Any change will be posted on this page with its date. Al-Zij is free: no ads, no data collection.
